YALDA is able to accept tax-deductible donations thanks to our fiscal sponsor, Ugly Duckling Presse. UDP is a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization, a NY State Registered Charity, and a member of the Community of Literary Magazines and Presses.

Two ways to donate

1. To make a secure, tax-deductible donation to YALDA online, please visit our Ugly Duckling partner page here. Be sure to write “For YALDA“ in the comments section. One-time, monthly and annual donations are all possible through UDP.

2. If you’d like to send a check, please make checks payable to “Ugly Duckling Presse” and be sure to write “YALDA donation” in your memo. Checks can be mailed to:

YALDA c/o Ugly Duckling Presse
The Old American Can Factory
232 Third Street, #E303
Brooklyn, NY 11215

What sponsors receive

All sponsors are considered vital members of the Young Artists Language and Devotion Alliance and will receive:

  • our annual digital Newsletter, which collects writing from all our poets and reports on the themes and fruits of our seasonal workshops
  • an invitation to our annual chapbook launch

Sponsors of $150 or more will also receive:

  • our annual spring May Nosegay, which collects poems from the chapbooks-in-progress as well as poetry by YALDA mentors and visiting writers

Sponsors of $250 or more will also receive:

  • recordings and resources from 8 seasonal workshops
  • copies of our 4 biennial printed chapbooks

YALDA is a labor of love and relies on contributions to continue and keep its services tuition-free.
We profoundly appreciate your support!
