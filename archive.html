One way YALDA endeavors to share out from our program to a wider audience is through our archives. Our archives include past issues of our annual publication and recordings of writing workshops by visiting writers and YALDA mentors, available for teachers and writers to enjoy and share freely. Our printed chapbooks have their own page.
