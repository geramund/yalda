Every two or so years we publish the chapbooks of the cohort graduating from their mentorship program. YALDAs are seminal in every step of the publication process and receive an apprenticeship in which they learn to design, print and bind their own chapbooks. The apprenticeship, and the celebratory launch reading that follows, are held in New York City.

Launching 22 August 2026, 5 PM — Ugly Duckling Presse Studio, The Old American Can Factory, Brooklyn.
